STEP 06

3kg Load Cell + mount
Hilitchi M3/M4/M5 bolts

After step 17 — cumulative 3D preview (replace with a real photo as you build)
#11 Luer HOLDER
Luer-to-Barb Adapters
21G Needles (GREEN hub)
Luer-lock Syringes
Hardware needed: 2× luer-to-1/8″ barb adapter + 1× Millex-FG 0.22 µm + 2× 21G luer-lock needles (GREEN hub) + 200 mm of 3 mm ID silicone tubing

How:

  1. Take Part #11 (luer holder). 2 large-bore through-holes side by side.
  2. Press barb adapter into LEFT hole. Barb out the BACK, female luer faces FRONT.

  4. Thread 21G needle onto front of barb adapter. Hand-tight.
  5. Thread 21G needle onto front of Millex-FG. Hand-tight.

  7. Push softened end onto the back barb of Part #11. Twist as you push.
Done when: Both luer parts firmly seated. Needles point same direction (down, parallel).

Hardware needed: 7×9 cm perfboard + ESP32 + HX711 + L298N + IRLZ44N + 1N5822 + LM2596 + 10-pos green screw terminal + piezo buzzer + red rocker switch + 12V/2A PSU

How:

  6. Mount perfboard inside Part #4 with 4× M3×8 standoffs.
  7. Plug in all 7 JST harnesses.
  8. Wire: barrel jack → 2A fuse → rocker → terminal block 12V → distribute.
  9. Flash firmware/gravimetric_dispenser.ino v0.3.0. DO NOT toggle the rocker yet.
Done when: All cables plugged. Rocker OFF. Nothing shorted (next step verifies).
STEP 27

Pre-power multimeter checks

After step 27 — cumulative 3D preview (replace with a real photo as you build)
Hardware needed: Multimeter

How:

  1. Rocker OFF, 12V UNPLUGGED, meter on Ω.
  2. +12V to GND: > 1 kΩ.
  3. +5V to GND: > 500 Ω.
  4. +3.3V to GND: > 500 Ω.
  5. MOSFET gate to source (GND): ~100 kΩ.
  6. Diode-test across 1N5822 flyback: stripe-side OL, anode-side ~0.3 V.
  7. Limit switch: push rod up → COM-NO short (< 1 Ω); rod down → OL.
Done when: All six checks pass. Fix any short BEFORE flipping rocker on.
STEP 28

First power-on + calibrate

After step 28 — cumulative 3D preview (replace with a real photo as you build)
Hardware needed: 10 g calibration weight (or 2 US nickels)

How:

  1. Plug in 12 V. Flip rocker ON.
  2. Serial Monitor 115200 → 'Firmware 0.3.0-2026-04-19'.
  3. HOMING runs: actuator retracts until limit switch trips, single beep.
  4. TFT shows DISPENSER 2.0 mL.
  5. HOLD encoder 2 s → CAL mode.
  6. Cradle empty → press encoder → tare.
  7. Place 10 g weight → press encoder → cal saved. Expect 10.0 ± 0.3 g on 3 kg cell.
  8. Remove weight → reading near 0.
Done when: Cal saved. Single boot beep heard. No errors.
STEP 29

⭐ NEW: Prime the fluid path with a syringe

After step 29 — cumulative 3D preview (replace with a real photo as you build)
#10 Cradle
Luer-lock Syringes
Vial Crimp Stoppers
Hardware needed: 1× luer-lock syringe + 5 mL DI water + empty 2R vial

How:

  1. Fill syringe with 5 mL DI water.
  2. Disconnect silicone tube from spike barb.
  3. Push syringe directly onto spike barb.
  4. Place empty 2R vial in cradle.
  5. Slowly push 3-4 mL through: syringe → barb → tube → solenoid (manually energize by touching coil leads to +12 V) → tube → adapter → water needle → vial.
  6. Watch tubing — solid water = primed.
  7. Pull syringe off. Reconnect silicone tube to spike barb.
Done when: No visible air bubbles in any tubing segment.
💡 Skipping this = first dispense will be off by 30-50%. Re-prime any time you see drift mid-run.
STEP 30

First wet test

After step 30 — cumulative 3D preview (replace with a real photo as you build)
#10 Cradle
Vial Crimp Stoppers
12V 2A PSU + Terminal
Hardware needed: Filled BAC vial + new 2R vial

How:

  1. Fill 30 mL BAC vial with DI water (NOT real bacteriostatic for test 1).
  2. Cap with BLUE crimp stopper.
  3. INVERT and push cap onto spike under BAC clamp. Small puff = punctured.
  4. Place fresh empty 2R vial (gray/black stopper) on cradle.
  5. Target = 1.0 mL. Encoder → confirm → GO.
  6. Watch: needles descend → solenoid clicks → cell reading climbs → ~0.97 g → solenoid closes → settle → 'DONE: 1.00 mL' → triple beep → needles retract → limit switch trips.
  7. Repeat 2 more times. Expect 1.00 mL ± 0.3 g on 3 kg cell.
Done when: 1.00 mL ± 0.3 g delivered. No leaks. BAC level dropped. Triple beep.
🎉 First dispense done.
